⚠ One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy PowerPyx and GTABase flag as potentially missable. In Chapter 1, the morning after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen — she says she is going to meditate. Follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. That session is gone after Chapter 1, and the game has no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter. It is unconfirmed whether that first session is required — doing it is the safe choice.
